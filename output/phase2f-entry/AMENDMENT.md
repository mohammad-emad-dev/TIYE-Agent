Keep the core free of child-process imports and preserve its schema/command rules.

worker exit/result and independent post-exit witness evidence govern success.

and their exits verified. No persisted PID adoption or resend is authorized.

worker and parent controller; it does not admit a general product dispatcher,

approval/retry/abandonment/takeover/Leader succession or release. No schema8 or
