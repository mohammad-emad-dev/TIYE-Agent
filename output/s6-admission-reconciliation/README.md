## First native attempt: required contract, still unimplemented

The next engineering deliverable is a concrete candidate acceptance contract.
Before a run it must identify the approved integration mechanism, exact candidate
and helper identities, model wire settings, effective environment and login-state
discovery behavior. Keep the existing account declaration; do not solicit secrets
or copy cached credentials into a mock profile. Decide credential provenance and
flow from the supported route before configuring it.

Specify one harmless task and its exact inputs, attempt count, startup/work/cleanup
budgets, expected protocol events, actual capture writer and bounded raw storage.
Define pass, fail and stop outcomes separately for transport, process cleanup,
storage and task delivery. Unexpected auth state, output, identity drift, storage
failure or unknown delivery must stop and preserve private evidence. No automatic
retry, duplicate launch, migration or API-billing substitution follows uncertainty.
Review raw evidence privately before publishing a field-allowlisted derivative.

For a proposed first bounded experiment, use a controlled single-owner workspace
and fresh run group. Power-loss recovery, hostile same-UID mutation, concurrent
deleters, secure erasure, automatic expiry and recovery after caller-record loss
remain outside the measured scope. Their exclusion requires an explicit review
of whether stop/quarantine is sufficient for this exact experiment. It does not
close P1/P2 or permit a product guarantee. Uncertain or missing final records
continue to require manual reconciliation; no purge is authorized here.
